# Code of Conduct

AfriTune aims to be a respectful, collaborative research community.

Participants are expected to engage constructively, welcome good-faith questions, credit others' work, and provide evidence-based technical feedback. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

If you experience or observe inappropriate conduct, contact the repository maintainers privately through an appropriate GitHub contact channel. Please avoid publishing sensitive reports in public issues. Maintainers may remove abusive content or restrict participation.

This is a project-specific community guideline, not an assertion of adoption of any external code-of-conduct license.
